About Palm 11

Distributed energy infrastructure for a changing Nigeria.

Palm 11 develops and integrates modern power systems for communities, estates, businesses and other energy users.

Who We Are

An energy infrastructure and distributed-energy development company.

Palm 11 Energy Limited develops and integrates distributed energy systems that combine solar PV, battery storage, hybrid generation and intelligent energy management.

Our focus is on the energy requirement first, then on the technology mix and delivery model required to address it. That means we can start with a 100 kW project, develop a community or commercial energy system, integrate multiple technologies, and progressively build larger energy infrastructure.

We work with a wide range of energy users — from estates and communities, to commercial and industrial facilities, property developers, institutions and development partners.

The Problem We Solve

Reliable distributed energy matters.

Across Nigeria, access to reliable and affordable electricity remains one of the biggest constraints on household wellbeing, business operations and economic development. Palm 11 exists to help close that gap — project by project — with energy systems designed for real-world conditions.

To develop and deliver distributed energy systems that give communities, businesses and developments access to reliable, increasingly clean and commercially sensible power.
