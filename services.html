Energy Solutions

One platform for multiple energy needs.

Palm 11's capabilities span distributed generation, storage, hybrid power and intelligent energy management — structured around the requirements of each project.

Capabilities

Integrated by design.

Technologies can be deployed individually or brought together as one system. The final architecture depends on the site's load profile, economics and operating conditions.

Distributed Energy Systems

Generation and storage, closer to the load.

Design and integration of distributed generation and storage systems that place reliable capacity closer to where energy is consumed — reducing losses, improving resilience and supporting scalability.

  • Distributed generation architecture
  • Solar PV + storage integration
  • Distribution and metering design
  • Site and demand assessment

  • Monitoring and performance support

Battery Energy Storage

BESS for reliability, load management and renewable integration.

Battery energy storage systems designed to improve reliability, reduce generator runtime, manage peak demand and increase the usable value of renewable generation.

  • Battery energy storage system (BESS) design
  • Peak shaving and load management
  • Backup and UPS-style architectures
  • Integration with solar and hybrid systems

Hybrid Power Systems

Solar, battery, gas/CNG and grid — working as one.

For sites that require a resilient generation mix, hybrid architectures combine renewable energy, storage and conventional generation (such as gas/CNG) where appropriate to the project.
